STB PLAY is a player. You need your own compatible portal and authorised access from your provider to load content.

STB PLAY is software for connecting to compatible portals and playing content that the user is authorised to access. STB PLAY does not provide, host, sell, select or distribute TV channels, movies, series, playlists, portals, subscriptions, accounts or login details.

  • You are responsible for using portal details and media access you are legally authorised to use.

  • STB PLAY is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by third-party portal providers, content owners, device brands or platforms.
  • Device and feature compatibility may differ by model, operating system and provider setup.
Quick answers

Need to know

Is STB PLAY free?

Yes. The app is free to download. STB PLAY does not charge for a membership, key or activation.

Does the app come with channels or movies?

No. STB PLAY does not provide media, channels, playlists or portal access. Add a compatible portal and use content you are authorised to access.

Which Android devices can I try?

The Android APK is intended for compatible phones, tablets, Android TV devices and TV boxes. Fire TV, Firestick and Meta Quest installation may need extra steps, and compatibility can vary.

Where do I enter the Downloader code?

Open the Downloader app on a supported TV device and enter 6068753. Then follow the prompts shown in the app.
